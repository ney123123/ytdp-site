One practice session, five moves.

It sits on top of the YouTube player you already use. Nothing to upload, no new app to learn.

  1. 1.0Loop

    Loop the hard part.

    Drag A and B onto the timeline and pick how many repeats you want. Tag moments as you go. YouTube chapters show up as tags automatically.

    A 1:12×3B 1:19
  2. 2.0Slow

    Slow it right down.

    Anywhere from 0.25× to 2×, without leaving the video. Learn it slow, then step the speed back up a notch at a time.

    0.25×0.5×0.75×1×
  3. 3.0Mirror

    Flip it to mirror.

    Face the screen and move the same way as the dancer. One tap flips only the video, not the player controls.

    LEFT⇋LEFT
  4. 4.0Record

    Film yourself doing it.

    Turn on your webcam and it records each loop while the video plays. The recording stays on your laptop. It never gets uploaded.

    REC 00:12

    Compare, rep by rep.

    Watch your take next to the original, synced. Step through each loop separately and spot exactly where you drift off the count.
