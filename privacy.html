Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

This policy covers the YouTube Dance Practice Chrome extension ("the extension").

What we collect

  • Google account ID and email address, when you sign in with Google. Used only to identify your account, run your free trial, and attach your purchase to your account.
  • Trial and purchase status (trial end date, whether you bought lifetime access, and the Stripe payment reference).

What we do not collect

  • Camera recordings. Recording and playback happen entirely on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Your YouTube viewing history, loops, tags, or settings. These stay in your browser's local extension storage.
  • Payment card details. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

Service providers

  • Google — sign-in.
  • Stripe — payment processing and receipts (Stripe Privacy Policy).
  • Amazon Web Services (Canada region) — stores your account record.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it for anything other than providing the extension.

Retention and deletion

Your account record is kept while your account exists. To delete it, email amztb12602@gmail.com from the address you signed in with; we delete it within 30 days. Payment records Stripe must keep for tax and legal reasons are retained by Stripe. Server logs (which may contain your account ID and payment reference) are kept for 14 days. Deleting your account record also removes your trial history.
